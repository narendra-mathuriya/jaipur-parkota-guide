Explorer Assistance

Frequently asked questions.

Practical tips on timings, navigation etiquette, pedestrian zones, and public accessibility in Jaipur.

Artisan workshops in Johari Bazar, Tripolia, and Kishanpole open around 11:00 AM and remain vibrant until 8:30 PM. For street breakfast (Samrat kachori, Sahu chai), early morning from 7:30 AM to 9:30 AM is ideal.
Yes. City Park (Mansarovar), Central Park, and Smriti Van feature dedicated paved circuits suitable for strollers. For wildlife outings, Nahargarh Biological Park offers electric battery carts.
Soni Ji Ka Rasta houses authorized hallmark and purity testing laboratories (Tunch labs). For gemstones, choose verified jewelers in Gopal Ji Ka Rasta providing IGI/GTL certifications.
Masala Chowk offers 21+ legendary street food stalls in one place. For historic standalone shops, Samrat in Chaura Rasta is famous for hing kachori, and Rawat Mishtan Bhandar is renowned for mawa and pyaz kachori.
Pacific Mall in Mansarovar is a highly anticipated 2026 project featuring over 450 global brands and Rajasthan's first IMAX. Manglam Pinkwalk in Jagatpura is another major upcoming lifestyle and entertainment hub.
Popular day trips include the historic Bhangarh Fort, the scenic Bisalpur Dam, the architectural marvel Chand Baori in Abhaneri, and the beautiful Kishangarh Marble Dump Yard.
